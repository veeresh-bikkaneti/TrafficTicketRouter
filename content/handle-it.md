# How to handle a traffic ticket (general information)

*Source for `site/learn.html`. Keep them in sync. Nebraska, verified 2026-09-20.
General information only — not legal advice.*

## Paying

- Minor traffic tickets can often be paid without appearing in court — **only if
  the citation is marked "waiver allowed."** Paying online is a waiver of
  appearance and a plea of guilty.
- Official: Nebraska Judicial Branch Internet Payment System —
  https://www.nebraska.gov/apps-courts-epayments/
- The DMV does not take ticket payments; its FAQ points to the court payment
  system — https://dmv.nebraska.gov/faq/how-can-i-pay-my-traffic-or-parking-ticket
- Do **not** pay online if a court appearance is required (DUI, driving on a
  suspended license, no insurance, and others).

## Appearing

- If the citation requires an appearance, or you want to contest it, you appear
  in the **county court** of the county where the stop happened.
- Hearing dates by date or last name: Nebraska Judicial Branch court calendars —
  https://nebraskajudicial.gov/e-services/court-calendars
  (calendars show scheduled hearings; they are not a ticket warehouse).

## Traffic school / dismissal options

- Some counties offer dismissal-on-completion programs for eligible minor
  violations. Eligibility is decided by the county attorney, not by this site.
- Ask the county court clerk what exists in your county.

## If you missed it (failure to appear)

- Missing a court date can add penalties, including a warrant or license
  suspension, depending on the charge. Do not ignore it.
- Call the county court clerk promptly. The numbers are on the county court
  cards in the check wizard.

## Getting help

- Nebraska Judicial Branch traffic self-help (instructions and forms):
  https://nebraskajudicial.gov/self-help/traffic
- Waiver rules and the statewide fine schedule:
  https://nebraskajudicial.gov/self-help/traffic/additional-information-traffic-cases-nebraska
- Find a lawyer: Nebraska Supreme Court Attorney Services Division directory —
  https://www.nefindalawyer.com
- NSBA Lawyer Referral (text, not a link): (402) 475-7091 in Lincoln,
  1-800-927-0117 toll-free.
