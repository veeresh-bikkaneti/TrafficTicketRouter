The fine print, in plain English

Legal

Not legal advice

Everything on this site is general information. Nothing here tells you what to do about your ticket, whether to contest it, or how it affects your insurance. For your situation, contact the court clerk or a licensed attorney.

Not a consumer report

This is not a consumer report and may not be used for employment, housing, credit, or insurance. We assemble no files on any person and serve no third parties — the site works the same for everyone and remembers no one.

We do not pull driving records

We link to the Nebraska DMV's official own-record request page. We never request, receive, or store anyone's motor vehicle record. The federal Driver's Privacy Protection Act and Nebraska's Uniform Motor Vehicle Records Disclosure Act restrict who may obtain those records and why; requesting your own record is a permitted use, and the official page explains the rules.

We hold no personal data

There is nowhere on this site to type a driver's license number, name, date of birth, plate, VIN, or title. We keep no accounts, no databases, and no analytics of who looked up what. There is nothing to breach, subpoena, or delete.

Self-check only

This tool is designed for checking your own situation. It is not a people-search product and must not be used to look into other people.

Official sources change

Court and DMV pages move. Every routed link carries a last-verified date, and automated checks run on a schedule. If a link is wrong, the entry is flagged, then disabled — a wrong confident link is worse than no link.
