We do not search tickets. We show you the official page.

There is no national ticket database. Every state keeps moving-violation records in its own courts and DMV. TicketRouter is a maintained directory of the official pages — court case search, citation payment, county courts, driving records, self-help — so you knock on the right door.

What this is not

  • Not a ticket search.

    We never query a court or DMV. You type your own information on the official site.

  • Not a record warehouse.

    No scraping, no stored records, no database of people.

  • Not legal advice.

    General information plus official links. Nothing here tells you what to do about your ticket.

  • Not people-search.

    There is nowhere on this site to type someone else's name. Self-check only.

Can a VIN, plate, or title find a speeding ticket?

What you haveCan it find a speeding ticket?
Citation numberYes — on the court's official site for that county.
Driver's licenseOnly on portals that explicitly offer DL search — Nebraska's JUSTICE searches by name, not DL.
NameSometimes — where the portal supports name search.
Plate numberOnly camera, parking, or toll violations, in some cities.
VIN / title / registrationNo. These identify the vehicle, not the driver. A speeding ticket is issued to a person.

How it works

  1. 1

    Tell us what you need — lost paper, your driving history, general how-to-handle-it info, or VIN/plate questions.

  2. 2

    Tell us where — state, then county.

  3. 3

    Get destination cards — the official pages, with fees, lags, and limitations spelled out. You finish the lookup there.

Coverage

TicketRouter covers all 50 states: county court cards for every county we could verify, plus statewide court search, citation payment, and DMV driving-record pages wherever those exist. The map shows 2,500+ courthouses — each pin pointing at the official court page. Every routed URL is verified against an official source. A few counties couldn't be verified; those are logged as gaps, never guessed.
