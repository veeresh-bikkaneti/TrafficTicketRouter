There is no national ticket database. Every state keeps moving-violation records in its own courts and DMV. TicketRouter is a maintained directory of the official pages — court case search, citation payment, county courts, driving records, self-help — so you knock on the right door.

What this is not

  • Not a ticket search. We never query a court or DMV. You type your own information on the official site.
  • Not a record warehouse. No scraping, no stored records, no database of people.
  • Not legal advice. General information plus official links. Nothing here tells you what to do about your ticket.
  • Not people-search. There is nowhere on this site to type someone else's name. Self-check only.

Can a VIN, plate, or title find a speeding ticket?

What you haveCan it find a speeding ticket?
Citation numberYes — on the court's official site for that county.
Driver's licenseSometimes — on portals that accept DL search.
NameSometimes — where the portal supports name search.
Plate numberOnly camera, parking, or toll violations, in some cities.
VIN / title / registrationNo. These identify the vehicle, not the driver. A speeding ticket is issued to a person.

How it works

  1. Tell us what you need — lost paper, your driving history, general how-to-handle-it info, or VIN/plate questions.
  2. Tell us where — state, then county. (Nebraska only in v1.)
  3. Get destination cards — the official pages, with fees, lags, and limitations spelled out. You finish the lookup there.

Coverage

v1 covers Nebraska: statewide court case search, citation payment, DMV driving records, and county cards for Lancaster, Douglas, and Sarpy counties. Every routed URL is verified against an official source. New states are added one verified file at a time.
